About Susan Lawrence

Bringing global expertise to your local real estate journey.

Susan Lawrence

Founder | Global Relocation Specialist | RE/MAX

Susan Lawrence is the founder of Susan Lawrence Real Estate, a globally connected real estate practice headquartered in Columbia, SC — with boots on the ground across the USA and trusted partnerships in New Zealand, Australia, the UK, Ireland, France, China, and beyond.

With deep expertise in residential transactions, international relocation, and student housing solutions, Susan helps clients transition smoothly — whether buying their first condo near University of South Carolina, relocating to Paris for work, or investing in a rental property in Auckland.

“A home isn’t just an address — it’s where life begins. I’m here to make sure yours starts right — no matter where ‘right’ is.”
